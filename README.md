# Even-Numbered Exercise Solutions for Stewart's Calculus (9th Edition)

A web-based platform providing detailed, step-by-step solutions to even-numbered exercises from **James Stewart's Calculus, 9th Edition**.

Link: https://dev-jzmn.github.io/math_solutions/index.html

## Motivation

Most STEM textbooks provide answers to odd-numbered exercises, leaving students with limited resources to verify their work on even-numbered problems.

Students often have to search online or rely on AI-generated solutions to check their answers. However, it's tedious and repetitive to find the solutions again when you want to review those to prepare for exams.

I developed this website to make even-numbered exercise solutions more accessible and to help students better understand the process of each solution.

Rather than simply providing final answers, the platform emphasizes **step-by-step explanations, helpful hints, and common mistakes** to support independent learning.

## Key Features

### Problem Search
- Quickly find solutions by exercise number.

### Step-by-Step Solutions
- Follow detailed mathematical explanations.
- Compare your own approach with the provided solution.

### Hints & Common Mistakes
- **Hints:** Get guidance before revealing the full solution.
- **Common Mistakes:** Learn about frequent errors and misconceptions.

### Issue Reporting & Solution Requests
- Report incorrect or unclear solutions.
- Request solutions for exercises not yet covered.
- Contribute feedback to improve the platform.
